THE FIGHT FOR THE GREAT SALT LAKE CONTINUES

The Great Salt Lake is dying. Water inflows have reduced to a trickle while our legislators spend water we don't have.

But there is hope.

With your support, we can reform our water usage and save our Great Salt Lake.

Youth Coalition for Great Salt Lake, or YCGSL for short, is a group of curious and passionate students working towards a sustainable future for our Great Salt Lake.
