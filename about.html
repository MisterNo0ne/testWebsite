ABOUT US

Youth Coalition for Great Salt Lake (YCGSL) is a group of high school and college age youth dedicated to saving Great Salt Lake and our future in Utah.
We do this through informing the public about the crisis facing Great Salt Lake, collaborating with other stakeholders, supporting effective legislation, and contributing to the community through service.
