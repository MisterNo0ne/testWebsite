Grow the Flow

Grow the Flow is harnessing the political and public will to restore Great Salt Lake by empowering citizens, identifying non-partisan solutions, and leading collaborative efforts with NGOs, the private sector, community members, institutions, and government leaders to get water to the Lake.

➜   Link to Website

Utah Rivers Council

Founded in 1994, the Utah Rivers Council is a non-profit organization that promotes sustainable aquatic ecosystems by protecting Utah’s watersheds and the communities they support.

➜   Link to Website

Sierra Club Utah Chapter

The Sierra Club Utah Chapter is working to secure immediate and long-term protections for the Great Salt Lake through grassroots organizing, direct action, legislative lobbying, and litigation.

➜   Link to Website

Great Salt Lake Audubon

Great Salt Lake Audubon, a chapter of the National Audubon Society, was founded in 1912 and is the oldest conservation organization in Utah. This volunteer organization strives to meet its mission through volunteer opportunities, seminars, educational field trips and informative general meetings with guest presenters.

➜   Link to Website

Save Our Great Salt Lake

Save Our Great Salt Lake is a group of organizers, artists, business owners, and concerned citizens working together to prevent ecosystem collapse at the Great Salt Lake.
